# Reference: values for the overview

Read this before you estimate an amount. The values change by law;
check the current year in an official source (BMF letters, the
Elster help) before you rely on one. No value here is tax advice.

## Expenses of an employee (Werbungskosten)

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag | 1,200 EUR (2022), 1,230 EUR (from 2023). Only expenses above it change the tax. |
| Entfernungspauschale | Per workday at the first workplace, one-way distance by the shortest road: 0.30 EUR/km for km 1 to 20, 0.38 EUR/km from km 21 (2022 to 2025). From 2026: 0.38 EUR from the first km (check). A trip out and back on different days counts once. Cap 4,500 EUR/year unless by own car. |
| Public transport | Ticket costs count instead of the allowance when they are higher (subscription, BahnCard used for the commute). |
| Homeoffice-Tagespauschale | 5 EUR/day, max. 600 EUR (2022); 6 EUR/day, max. 1,260 EUR (from 2023). Days at the workplace do not count, unless no other workplace was available. |
| Work equipment | Net price up to 800 EUR: full deduction in the year of purchase. Computers and software: one year of use since 2021, so full deduction. Mixed private use: the advisor may take 50 %. |
| Telefon/Internet | 20 % of the bills, max. 20 EUR/month, without single proof; keep the bills of three months. |
| Kontoführungspauschale | 16 EUR/year without proof (practice of the tax offices). |
| Business trips | Verpflegung inland: 14 EUR for the day out and the day back and for days over 8 h, 28 EUR for full days. A provided breakfast cuts 5.60 EUR, lunch or dinner 11.20 EUR. Subtract what the employer paid. |
| Moving for the job | Moving closer so the daily commute (out and back) gets at least 1 h shorter. Transport costs with receipts plus the Pauschale für sonstige Umzugsauslagen (BUKG; 964 EUR for the entitled person from 03/2024, more per further household member; earlier years lower, check). |
| Doctoral thesis | Printing, binding, fees and literature are expenses when the thesis belongs to the job. |

## Other parts

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Capital income | Sparer-Pauschbetrag 801 EUR (2022), 1,000 EUR (from 2023) per person. Without withheld tax the certificate changes nothing, but the checklist asks for it. |
| Vorsorgeaufwendungen | Basic health and care insurance always count. Other insurances (liability, accident, car) only up to 1,900 EUR for employees together with health insurance, so for most employees they change nothing. |
| Donations | Only to tax-privileged receivers. Up to 300 EUR the bank or payment statement is proof; above that a Zuwendungsbestätigung. |
| Health costs | Count only above the zumutbare Belastung (1 to 7 % of income). |
| Household services | 20 % of the labour cost, max. 4,000 EUR; craftsmen 20 %, max. 1,200 EUR. Tenants need the amounts in the Nebenkostenabrechnung or a landlord certificate. No flat rate. |

## Deadlines

- Voluntary return (Antragsveranlagung): four years after the end of
  the tax year, for 2022 until 31.12.2026.
- A refund estimate: expenses above the Pauschbetrag times the
  marginal rate (roughly 30 to 35 % at a middle income). Give it to
  the user only, never in the overview.

## Pitfalls seen

- The same booking can arrive twice (booking and cancellation); count
  the net amount.
- A shop that sells books also sells novels; a rail ticket can be a
  holiday. Read the item lines and ask.
- A sponsoring platform payment looks like a donation and is none.
- Payment provider receipts without a payee in the subject hide
  person-to-person payments; most are private.
- Mail folders are often wrong: certificates in a membership folder,
  invoices in the inbox. Search all folders, then report the misfiled
  ones.
